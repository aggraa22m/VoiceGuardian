# VoiceGuardian — Your Celestial AI Companion

VoiceGuardian is an emotionally intelligent, voice-first AI designed to provide a safe, calm space for mental health check-ins, focus coaching, and empathetic support.

## ✨ Key Features

*   **Real-Time Voice Communion**: Powered by Gemini Live API for human-like, low-latency conversation.
*   **Celestial Presence**: A reactive, visual orb that pulses and emits energy spikes synchronized with the AI's voice.
*   **Four Emotional Modes**:
    *   **Empathetic Friend**: For general venting and emotional support.
    *   **Elder Care Assistant**: Focused on patience and gentle reminders.
    *   **Focus Coach**: Designed to help you stay on task and manage ADHD/distractions.
    *   **Crisis Support**: Provides a grounded, calming presence during high-stress moments.
*   **End-to-End Presence**: Secure processing with live transcription so you can see what is being said.

## 🚀 How to Use

1.  **Select Your Mode**: Before starting, tap one of the mode buttons (e.g., "CARE TAKER" or "FOCUS COACH") at the bottom of the interface to set the Guardian's personality.
2.  **Initiate Communion**: Tap the large microphone icon labeled "Begin Communion."
3.  **Grant Permissions**: Allow the browser to access your microphone when prompted.
4.  **Speak Naturally**: Once the orb begins to pulse softly (teal/indigo), the Guardian is listening. 
5.  **Watch the Presence**: When the Guardian speaks, the orb will emit white glowing spikes and expand based on the volume of the voice.
6.  **Read Along**: A transcript area at the bottom provides a scrolling history of your conversation.
7.  **Rest the Presence**: Tap the stop icon to end the session and clear the connection.

## 💡 Interaction Tips

*   **Be Patient**: The Guardian is designed to wait for you to finish your thought. You don't need to rush.
*   **Concise Wisdom**: The Guardian is programmed to give brief, impactful advice (1-2 sentences) to keep the conversation focused on you.
*   **Privacy First**: Your audio is processed in real-time. No recordings are stored locally by this application.
