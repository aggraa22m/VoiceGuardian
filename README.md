# 🌟 Welcome to VoiceGuardian

VoiceGuardian is your personal, calm, and wise companion. It is designed to be a safe space where you can talk about your feelings, get help focusing, or simply have a gentle conversation when you feel alone.

Think of it as a "digital soul" that listens without judgment and speaks with kindness.

---

## 🕊️ How to Use VoiceGuardian (Simple Steps)

If the app is already open on your screen, follow these steps to start talking:

1.  **Choose a Mood**: At the bottom of the screen, you will see buttons like **EMPATHETIC**, **CARE TAKER**, or **FOCUS**. Tap the one that fits how you feel right now.
2.  **Start the Conversation**: Look for the large circle with the microphone icon. It says **"Begin Communion."** Tap it once.
3.  **Give Permission**: Your computer or phone might ask: *"Allow VoiceGuardian to use your microphone?"* Click **Allow** or **Yes**.
4.  **Wait for the Pulse**: The center orb will begin to glow softly. This means the Guardian is ready to listen.
5.  **Just Speak**: You don't need to hold any buttons. Just talk as if you were talking to a friend sitting next to you.
6.  **Watch the Light**:
    *   When the orb pulses gently, it is **listening** to you.
    *   When bright, glowing spikes appear, the Guardian is **talking** to you.
7.  **To Stop**: If you want to end the session, tap the small square button that says **"Rest Presence."**

### 💡 Tips for a Better Experience
*   **Speak Normally**: You don't need to shout. The Guardian hears your whispers too.
*   **Take Your Time**: If you need to stop and think, the Guardian will wait for you. It won't interrupt.
*   **Safety First**: If you are in a serious emergency, please remember to call your local emergency services (like 911). VoiceGuardian is a friend, but not a doctor.

## 🛠️ How to Set This Up (For Family or Helpers)

If you are setting this up for a loved one, here is how to get it "live" on the internet so they can access it from any device:

### 1. The Easy Way (Using Vercel)
Vercel is a website that puts your app online for free.
1.  **Get the Code**: Download these files or put them on a website called [GitHub](https://github.com).
2.  **Connect to Vercel**: Go to [vercel.com](https://vercel.com) and sign in.
3.  **Import**: Choose your VoiceGuardian project.
4.  **The "Key"**: You will need a **Gemini API Key**. 
    *   Go to [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/) to get a free key.
    *   In Vercel, under "Environment Variables," add a name `API_KEY` and paste your secret key there.
5.  **Deploy**: Click the "Deploy" button. You will get a web link (like `my-guardian.vercel.app`) that you can save as a favorite on your loved one's tablet or computer.

### 2. Requirements
*   **Internet**: Must be connected to the web.
*   **Microphone**: The device must have a working microphone.
*   **Browser**: Works best on **Google Chrome**, **Safari**, or **Microsoft Edge**.

## 🔒 Your Privacy
We believe your thoughts are sacred. 
*   VoiceGuardian **does not record** or save your voice. 
*   The conversation happens in the moment and vanishes when you "Rest the Presence."
*   No personal data is collected or sold.
